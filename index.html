/ RESEARCH

My current work on on-policy distillation examines where a teacher model improves a student's reasoning, and whether weaker teachers can still provide useful supervision.

I also study multi-agent coordination when agents have different information and limited ways to communicate. On the efficiency side, I work on compressing the KV caches passed between agents while preserving information they need for the task.

Alongside these projects, I explore LLMs for quantum computing. My earlier work focused on communication efficiency, conflicting client gradients and knowledge distillation in federated learning.

01New on arXiv

Demystifying On-Policy Distillation: Where Does the Teacher's Advantage Live?

Li, Y., Zhao, F., Hou, Z., An, Z., & Du, W.

arXiv preprint · October 2026

We study OPD from a knowledge-distillation perspective: which teacher corrections help a student reason better? By changing tokens while keeping model weights fixed, we find that the teacher's advantage accumulates across many corrections, and that choosing where to intervene matters.

Sanzaru: Benchmarking Protocol Formation under Epistemic & Communication Asymmetry

Li, Y., An, Z., & Du, W.

arXiv preprint · October 2026

Can agents work together when they cannot all see, hear, or speak? SANZARU tests this with 120 cooperative bomb-defusal puzzles. Across seven models, about 60% of team failures stem from communication.

03New on arXiv

Neither True nor False: Do LLMs Represent Truth-Value Gaps in Plural Predication?

Nakshbandi, D., An, Z., Li, Y., Giri, S., & Du, W.

arXiv preprint · October 2026

Some statements are neither true nor false. We test whether LLMs recognize this in plural descriptions using 1,000 diagnostic examples. Across seven open-weight models, we find a tendency to force these cases into binary judgments.

Compressing KV-Cache Communication in Multi-Agent Systems with Collective Compensation

Li, Y., An, Z., & Du, W.

Under review, ICLR 2027

Orthogonal BackFill (OBF) compresses the KV cache passed between agents by folding information from discarded values into the retained cache. It reduces per-agent relay volume by approximately 4.7× compared with full-cache relay. At the same relay budget, OBF outperforms eviction alone in 31 of 42 comparisons across seven benchmarks and Qwen3 models from 4B to 14B.

cOPD: Contrastive On-Policy Distillation via Dual-Branch Supervision

Hou, Z., Zhao, F., Li, Y., An, Z., & Du, W.

Under review, ICLR 2027

Can a weaker teacher still help train a student? cOPD investigates this question through contrastive supervision for on-policy distillation.

FedSparse: A Communication-Efficient Federated Learning Framework Based on Sparse Updates

Li, J., Zhang, Y., Li, Y., Gong, X., & Wang, W.

Electronics, 13(24), 5042

FedSparse uses historical gradients to identify important parameters and applies stronger sparsity penalties to less important updates. This reduces communication by up to 24% while keeping accuracy nearly unchanged.

HybridCom: Improve Federated Learning Efficiency on Unstable Data

Li, J., Zhang, Y., Li, Y., Gong, X., & Wang, W.

IEEE International Conference on Communications (ICC), pp. 2622–2627

When a client's data distribution changes, the global model may not yet reflect that change. HybridCom combines local performance and server-side gradient signals to prioritize these clients, improving accuracy by approximately 1.3% over the evaluated baselines.

Gradient Calibration for Non-IID Federated Learning

Li, J., Zhang, Y., Li, Y., Gong, X., & Wang, W.

2nd ACM Workshop on Data Privacy and Federated Learning Technologies for Mobile Edge Network, pp. 119–124

Federated Gradient Tailor corrects conflicting client gradients before aggregation. On non-IID MNIST and CIFAR-10, it improves accuracy by 3.2–5.8 percentage points over FL without gradient calibration and reaches baseline accuracy in 2–6× fewer communication rounds. The paper also provides a convergence proof.

Paper

DCD: A New Framework for Distillation Learning with Dynamic Curriculum

Li, J., Zhang, Y., Li, Y., Gong, X., & Wang, W.

IEEE HPCC/DSS/SmartCity/DependSys, pp. 928–935

DCD uses feedback from the teacher and student to adjust the order of training samples during distillation. Compared with random sampling, it improves accuracy by 1.7% on CIFAR-10, 2.5% on CIFAR-100 and 1% on CINIC-10.

Research

I work with Wan Du at UC Merced. Previously, I worked remotely with Yuchao Zhang at the joint laboratory of BUPT and ChuangCache.

LLM Post-Training, Multi-Agent Systems & Quantum Circuits

University of California, Merced — Research Assistant, advised by Dr. Wan Du

  • Orthogonal BackFill (OBF). I developed a method to recover complementary information from discarded KV values and fold it into the cache passed to the next agent, without increasing the retained cache size.
  • SANZARU. I developed a benchmark of 120 bomb-defusal puzzles to test how agents coordinate when they have different information and communication constraints.
  • Demystifying on-policy distillation. I designed token-flip experiments to test where a teacher improves student reasoning, comparing teacher substitutions with student resampling while keeping model weights fixed.
  • cOPD. I contributed to the research motivation and experimental design, exploring whether weaker teachers can provide useful contrastive supervision for on-policy distillation.
  • LLMs for quantum computing. I am investigating encoder-based approaches to fault-tolerant circuit synthesis. I also audit and correct Qiskit QuantumKatas and add paired multiple-choice tasks to distinguish coding errors from errors in quantum reasoning.

Communication Efficiency & Conflict in Federated Learning

Joint Laboratory of BUPT and ChuangCache — Research Assistant (Remote), advised by Dr. Yuchao Zhang

  • I designed core algorithms, implemented experiments and built the shared evaluation framework used across our four federated-learning papers.
  • FedSparse. I designed the Resource Optimization Proximal loss to encourage sparse client updates, using historical gradients to preserve important updates while reducing communication.
  • Federated Gradient Tailor. I built the method for correcting conflicting client gradients before aggregation and wrote its formal convergence proof.
  • HybridCom. I developed a client-prioritization method that combines local performance with server-side gradient signals to account for changing data distributions.
  • DCD. I developed a curriculum that uses teacher and student feedback to adjust the order of training samples during knowledge distillation.
